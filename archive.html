3D-printed RC SU-27

From February through summer 2026, I worked from an existing model to slice, print, and assemble an RC SU-27. I tuned material and print settings, bonded the airframe, and installed the EDF, ESC, battery, receiver, and servos.

Power, control-surface, and thrust tests have run. The aircraft remains unfinished and has not flown, so this is a fabrication and ground-test project rather than a successful flight claim. A material-choice mistake became the main source of rework.

Online English reading tutoring

Since September 2026, I have co-taught a large Grade 4 class in Honghe, Yunnan, online with another volunteer. I have completed three one-hour lessons and prepared lesson plans and presentation materials.

Lesson plans, slides, and teacher feedback are preserved. With only three sessions and no measured learning outcome, I record the service and preparation without claiming an academic improvement.
